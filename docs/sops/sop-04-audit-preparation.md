# SOP-QA-004 — Audit preparation

## Purpose
Keep the site continuously ready for regulatory inspection.

## Readiness indicators
Audit readiness is measured weekly: count of open critical deviations, count of overdue CAPAs, and batches awaiting Qualified Person sign-off. All three should trend toward zero.

## Evidence packs
For each open critical or major deviation, an evidence pack is maintained: the deviation record, investigation, linked CAPAs with status, and sign-off history.

## Mock audits
A mock audit is run each quarter against a rotating area. Findings become audit findings in the QA system with severity and due dates.

## During inspection
Inspectors receive read-only access to deviation, CAPA, and batch records. The agent's answers during an inspection always cite the underlying records.
