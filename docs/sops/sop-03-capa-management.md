# SOP-QA-003 — CAPA management

## Purpose
Ensure corrective and preventive actions address root causes and complete on time.

## Linkage
Every CAPA links to the deviation that triggered it. A CAPA for a major deviation must be at least in progress before the related batch can advance in the release chain.

## Ownership and dates
Each CAPA has a named owner and a due date. The owner updates status weekly: open, in progress, or closed.

## Overdue handling
A CAPA past its due date becomes overdue and blocks the QA manager sign-off for every batch linked to its deviation. Overdue CAPAs are reviewed in the weekly quality meeting until closed.

## Effectiveness check
Closed CAPAs include an effectiveness check: the same root cause must not recur within the monitoring window, or the CAPA is reopened.
