# SOP-QA-001 — Deviation handling

## Purpose
Define how deviations from approved procedures are recorded, classified, and investigated.

## Recording
Every deviation is recorded in the QA system within one business day of discovery, with a factual title, a description of what happened versus what should have happened, and the affected batch.

## Classification
Deviations are classified critical, major, or minor using the classification rules:
critical covers sterility, data-integrity, or patient-safety impact; major covers validation gaps and specification failures; minor covers documentation-only issues with no product impact. Repeats escalate one level.

## Investigation
Critical and major deviations require a documented root-cause investigation before closure. Minor deviations may close with a correction and a preventive note.

## Closure
A deviation closes only when its investigation is approved and any linked CAPA is at least in progress. Critical-deviation closure requires QA manager confirmation.
